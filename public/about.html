Our Journey

The History of MD Care

From a modest clinic to a multi-specialty healthcare leader, our journey is defined by continuous growth, innovation, and an unwavering commitment to our patients.

2008

The Foundation

MD Care Hospital opened its doors as a dedicated 50-bed community clinic, laying the groundwork for accessible, high-quality medical care in Jalandhar.

2015

Expansion & Specialization

Transformed into a multi-specialty institution with the launch of our advanced Cardiology and Oncology wings, integrating state-of-the-art diagnostic imaging.

2020

Level 1 Trauma Center

Inaugurated our specialized Trauma Center and NICU, establishing a robust, round-the-clock emergency response network equipped for critical care.
